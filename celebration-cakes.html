Questions

How much notice do you need?
Please allow 1 week. A more detailed design can need longer, so ask early if you can.
Can you work around an allergy?
In most cases, yes. Tell me about the allergy or intolerance in the quote form and we will talk it through.
Do you deliver?
Yes, in Sugar Land, Missouri City and Sienna. The delivery fee depends on the distance. You can also pick the cake up by appointment.
Can you ship a cake?
No. The cakes are too delicate to ship.
Does the cake need a fridge?
If it won't be served within two hours of pickup or delivery, yes: it is made with real butter, milk and eggs. Take it out of the fridge at least two to three hours before serving. It tastes best at room temperature.

Have a date in mind?

Tell me a little about the occasion and I will come back to you with a quote.
