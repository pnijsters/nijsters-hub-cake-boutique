Wedding questions

How far ahead should we book?
Please allow 1 month. Send the quote form as soon as you have your date.
Can we taste the cake first?
Yes. We arrange it ahead of time: I put together a box of cake flavors and fillings, and you try them at home whenever it suits you.
Do you deliver to the venue?
Yes, in Sugar Land, Missouri City and Sienna. The delivery fee depends on the distance. [I set the cake up at the venue.]
Can you work around an allergy?
In most cases, yes. Tell me about the allergy or intolerance in the quote form and we will talk it through.
Does the cake need a fridge?
If it won't be served within two hours of delivery, yes: it is made with real butter, milk and eggs. Take it out of the fridge at least two to three hours before serving. It tastes best at room temperature.

Have a date in mind?

Tell me a little about the occasion and I will come back to you with a quote.
