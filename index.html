Meet Chef Ellah

Leonila "Ellah" Nijsters has made cakes professionally since 2013, first for the expat community in Singapore, then as a cottage bakery in Denver, and since 2021 from her home studio in Sugar Land.

She holds a Culinary Arts degree from Johnson & Wales University, where she graduated summa cum laude as a pastry chef[, and a master's certificate from the Knightsbridge School of Cake Decorating]. Each order is made fresh, by hand.
